Text: Opening a bank account? Bring these: 1. A photo ID. Some banks also accept a foreign passport or consular ID. 2. Your SSN. Some banks and credit unions accept an ITIN instead. 3. Proof of address. Call ahead and ask what they accept. It can save you a second trip.

3. ENCOURAGING | topic: a past bank problem is one chapter | no image, no link
Text: A past bank problem is one chapter, not your whole story. Lots of people have had an account closed. Many find a way back in. If you're working on it right now, you're already doing the hard part. Keep going.

4. BANKING WORD OF THE WEEK (series start, week of 2026-11-16) | word: Overdraft (CFPB definition, opt-in rule) | image tips/2026-11-16/letmebank-word-overdraft.png | no link
Text: Banking word of the week: Overdraft. An overdraft happens when you don't have enough money in your account to cover a transaction. Good to know: banks can't charge overdraft fees on ATM and most debit card transactions unless you agreed to it (opted in).

5. LINK POST | topic: Early Warning Services is another banking report | image tips/2026-11-16/letmebank-ews-another-banking-report.png | link https://letmebank.com/early-warning-services/
Text: ChexSystems isn't the only report a bank may check. Early Warning Services (EWS) tracks checking and savings history, like accounts closed over unpaid fees. It doesn't cover loans or credit cards. Denied and not sure why? Ask which report they used.
